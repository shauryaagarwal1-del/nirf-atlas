## Step 8: Ranks, ties and rank bands

**Rows:** 11,647 -> 11,647

**What changed:** rank_status: exact 6,971, band 4,671, lettered 3, withdrawn 2. `rank` is an integer for exact ranks; 333 rows share their rank with another institution (`is_tie`). Banded rows keep `rank` empty: NIRF gives only the band, and we never invent a mid-point. Every row with a known position gets a standard `rank_band` (1-10, 11-25, 26-50, 51-100, 101-150, 151-200, 201-300); NIRF bands like 51-75 or 102-125 fall inside one standard band. 121 banded rows (11-50) span two standard bands and keep NIRF's band. `in_top10/50/100` use the exact rank, or the band's upper limit for banded rows (a 76-100 row is in the top 100 for sure). 'Lettered' ranks ('21A', '26A', '16A' in 2018) keep their number: each one's score is just above the plain rank with the same number (e.g. 21A = 53.24 vs rank 21 = 53.16), so NIRF slotted them in without renumbering; they are flagged so the team can revisit this choice. 'Rank Withdrawn' rows get no rank.

**Why:** Rank is the main measure in most of the 40 questions. Keeping rank_raw shows exactly what NIRF printed; the clean numeric rank and the bands make 'top 50', 'moved from 51-100 into the top 50' (Q7.1) and 'same band next year' (Q7.3) simple filters.
